**Engagement** is what we do. It is delivered through eight **Domains**. Each domain has **Products**. Customers buy **Apps**: a product combined with the **Deliverables** around it.

## The Levels

| Level | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Engagement** | The whole process. Moving organisations from systems of record to systems of engagement. |
| **Domain** | One of eight clusters of thought, method, frameworks and models. A domain is a way of seeing a problem. |
| **Product** | The core of what we offer inside a domain. |
| **Deliverable** | What surrounds a product: advisory, consulting, training, education and other services. |
| **App** | What the customer buys. A product combined with the deliverables around it. |

The rule: **customers buy apps.** An app is never just software. It is a product plus the services that make it work for that customer.

Engagement is the whole process. [[Engage]] is one of the eight domains inside it: the archetype layer that tells every other domain who it is talking to.

## Reveal

Reveal makes the invisible visible. Every app in this domain does the same three things: asks a short set of plain questions, scores the answers, and hands back a report that shows the respondent something they could not see before. The report is the door. The conversation that follows is where the deliverables come in.

### Ready

The question: **are your leads actually sales-ready, or just leads?** One lead engine, three results: Volume, Quality and Insight, overlapping into sales-ready leads.

- **SalesReady** - a 10-question self-assessment of lead readiness. Each answer is scored 1 to 5 and banded Critical, High or Elevated. The respondent gets a results screen and a personalised PDF report. Around it: a read of the results, a working session on the gaps, and help closing them. The same engine can be rewritten around a partner's own domain: their questions, their brand. See [[Sales-Ready]].
- **LeadReady** - the second app on the Ready product, grown from the original DEAL diagnostic (Dynamic Engagement with Active Leads).

### Navigation

The question: **what is your website actually doing for you, and where is it leaking?**

- **Website** - a forensic review against a 12-dimension Website Analysis Framework, scored out of 120 and shown as a spider chart. Three uses: prospect analysis, client analysis, competitor analysis. A self-service version, around 20 plain-language questions with an instant spider chart and benchmark, is scoped.

## Intelligence

Intelligence turns noise into something a business can act on. The flow runs Data, Information, Knowledge, Insight, Wisdom, then Impact. In plain terms: aggregate, curate, syndicate.

### Intelligence

The product: the platform and a source set tuned to the customer's world. Each app is that product built for one community or sector, with the editorial judgement that decides what matters delivered around it. Newsletter, alerts, reports and feeds are outputs of the app, not separate apps.
