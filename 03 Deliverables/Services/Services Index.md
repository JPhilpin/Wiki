Services sit beside the eight domains, not inside them. They plug in underneath any app. An app plus services is a solution.

6 pages.

- [[Advisory]] - Our read and recommendation, at board or leadership level.
- [[Consulting]] - Hands-on work on the customer's problem. Usually where a new engagement starts.
- [[Enablement]] - Getting the customer's people and systems ready to use what they have bought.
- [[Training]] - Teaching people to use the app.
- [[Workshops]] - Structured sessions that work a problem through with the customer's team.
- [[Education]] - Teaching people the thinking behind it.

See [[Product Hierarchy]].
