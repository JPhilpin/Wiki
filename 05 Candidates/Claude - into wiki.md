Claude - into wiki

The best persuasion architecture models for B2B sales and marketing combine behavioral psychology, structured discovery, and narrative frameworks to guide a buyer toward a commercial decision. [[1](https://marketing.org.nz/hubfs/The%20Persuasion%20Pathway.pdf), [2](https://www.smartinsights.com/persuasion-marketing/)]

Core Persuasion Architecture Models

- **Cialdini's Principles of Influence:** Uses six psychological triggers--[Reciprocity, Commitment, Consensus, Liking, Authority, and Scarcity](https://www.janek.com/blog/the-psychology-of-persuasion-in-b2b-sales-negotiations/)--to shape messaging, web design, and negotiation tactics. [[1](https://www.janek.com/blog/the-psychology-of-persuasion-in-b2b-sales-negotiations/), [2](https://www.smartinsights.com/persuasion-marketing/)]
- **The Persuasion Pathway:** A neurochemical story-driven model that moves an audience through emotional stages--from initial cortisol-spiking tension (Catalyst) to oxytocin-building empathy and a final perspective shift. [[1](https://marketing.org.nz/hubfs/The%20Persuasion%20Pathway.pdf)]
- **SPIN Selling:** Employs a structured sequence of Situation, Problem, Implication, and Need-Payoff questions to help prospects uncover the hidden costs of their challenges. [[1](https://inaccord.com/blog-posts/a-simplified-overview-of-the-5-most-effective-b2b-sales-methodologies), [2](https://www.amplemarket.com/blog/15-proven-methodologies-to-close-deals-and-build-better-b2b-relationships)]
- **The Challenger Sale:** Focuses on teaching, tailoring, and taking control of the conversation by disrupting the buyer's current assumptions with fresh insights. [[1](https://www.amplemarket.com/blog/15-proven-methodologies-to-close-deals-and-build-better-b2b-relationships), [2](https://inaccord.com/blog-posts/a-simplified-overview-of-the-5-most-effective-b2b-sales-methodologies)]
- **Solution & Value Selling:** Aligns the pitch directly to measurable ROI and specific customer pain points, ensuring the product is viewed as a necessary fix rather than an extra expense. [[1](https://www.amplemarket.com/blog/15-proven-methodologies-to-close-deals-and-build-better-b2b-relationships), [2](https://inaccord.com/blog-posts/a-simplified-overview-of-the-5-most-effective-b2b-sales-methodologies)]

How to Apply Them in B2B

- **Marketing (Top-of-Funnel):** Use Cialdini's social proof and content marketing authority alongside narrative hooks (The Persuasion Pathway) to capture attention and build early familiarity. 
- **Sales Discovery (Mid-Funnel):** Implement SPIN questioning to let prospects articulate their own pain points and value requirements.
- **Closing & Qualification (Bottom-of-Funnel):** Apply Challenger insights to overcome status-quo bias and justify commercial value. [[1](https://clutch.co/resources/psychology-of-marketing-b2b-audiences)]
