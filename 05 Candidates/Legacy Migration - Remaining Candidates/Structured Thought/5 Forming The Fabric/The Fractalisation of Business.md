    <p>🚧 I have always thought about the world of marketing as three interlocking circles</p>
    <p><strong>you have to make stuff:</strong> websites, emails, collateral, case studies, data sheets ... whatever ... but that is not the job.</p>
    <p><strong>yes - you then have to use that stuff</strong> to get you in front of whoever you have defined as your audience ... the CEO, the Buyer, the 'Drinks Industry'.</p>
    <p>.. but it’s the third part that gets missed - let's call it the infrastructure.</p>
    <p> Turns out that if you think and plan ahead, you can actually build tecnology infrastructure and a pretty low cost. No - not running your newsletter on ghostm youe web site on Wordpress and your CRM in Hubspot. Because that brings you all a whole host of different problems - because they are all fighting with each other over where the single source of truth resides. (None of them by the way.)</p>
    <p> Because the infrastructure is nothing to do with the tech stack and all to do with how you think about your data - both structured and non structured - and also understand that it is not just about customer data, financial data and all the rest of it - but the **data of marketing**. Just as you might think about how an org can be broken down into larger and larger number so data nodes - but so can everything.
