## Then and Now

## The Original Pillar Map and the Business Equation Side by Side

🚧 Insert two images side by side

Before there was *The Business Equation*, there was this: eight core ideas that explained how organisations actually work.  

They began as a simple sketch - a way to link value, people, and performance - and over time became the framework that still anchors everything today.  

The 8 Pillars describe not what a business does, but **how it thinks**.

## The 8 Pillars

When organisations lose sight of value, they drift toward activity for activity’s sake - busy, not better. 

Value is the compass.

**[[Structured Thinking/📙 Definitions/Language]]** ...

... shapes how we communicate and understand business concepts. 

The words we choose matter.  

Language isn’t decoration - it’s architecture. 

It frames thought, signals intent, and determines whether people align or scatter.

</div>

<div class="pillar-section">
<div class="pillar-image-left">

![[Invisibles/_Images/pf-tenets/pillars/learning.png]]

</div>

**[[Structured Thinking/📙 Definitions/Learning]]** ...

... is continuous improvement and adaptation. 

Organizations must evolve.  

But learning isn’t just training - it’s curiosity formalised. 

The moment we treat it as compliance, we kill it. 

Real learning loops insight back into action.

</div>

<div class="pillar-section">
<div class="pillar-image-right">

![[Invisibles/_Images/pf-tenets/pillars/commerce.png]]

</div>

**[[Structured Thinking/📙 Definitions/Commerce]]** ...

... is the exchange of value between parties. 

It's how business happens.  

Healthy commerce isn’t extraction; it’s collaboration. 

The best deals create shared momentum, not zero-sum winners.

</div>

<div class="pillar-section">
<div class="pillar-image-left">

![[Invisibles/_Images/pf-tenets/pillars/work.png]]

</div>

**[[Structured Thinking/📙 Definitions/Work]]** ...

... is how we create and deliver value. 

Understanding workflows matters.  

Work reveals culture. 

The way tasks flow - or jam - tells you more about leadership than any vision statement ever will.

</div>

<div class="pillar-section">
<div class="pillar-image-right">

![[Invisibles/_Images/pf-tenets/pillars/technology.png]]

</div>

**[[Structured Thinking/1 Atoms/Technology]]** ...

... enables and transforms how we work and create value.  


It’s neither saviour nor villain - it’s leverage. 

When designed around people, it expands potential. When not, it amplifies dysfunction.

</div>

<div class="pillar-section">
<div class="pillar-image-left">

**[[Structured Thinking/📙 Definitions/Data]]** ...

... informs decisions and reveals patterns. It's the new currency.  

But raw data means little without context. 

Insight isn’t found in dashboards - it’s found in dialogue. Data helps us see; people help us understand.

</div>

Getting it right isn’t about control; it’s about trust.
