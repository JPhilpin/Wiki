<h2>How AI Reshuffles The Knowledge Economy</h2>
<p>Artificial intelligence isn't just a productivity tool—it's fundamentally restructuring how knowledge flows, gets valued, and creates competitive advantage. In this episode, Dani brings together two leading thinkers on platforms, ecosystems, and the future of work to explore what AI means for the knowledge economy.</p>
<p>Sangeet Paul Choudary has spent two decades mapping how platforms reshape business models and value creation. Rishad Tobaccowala is an author and speaker on the future of marketing and technology in an AI-driven world. Together, they examine the seismic shifts underway.</p>
<h3>What You'll Explore</h3>
<ul>
<li>How AI is collapsing knowledge hierarchies and creating new forms of value</li>
<li>Why platform thinking is essential for navigating an AI-native economy</li>
<li>The role of community and collaboration in a world of artificial intelligence</li>
<li>How individuals and organizations can position themselves for the next wave</li>
</ul>
