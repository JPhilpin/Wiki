## According To Claude

**wiki.philpin.com** is the personal wiki of John Philpin, a business strategist and advisor who specialises in what he calls "Structured Thought" - a methodology he uses to help organisations navigate complexity and change. The wiki serves as a knowledge repository and collection of his latest entries, notes, and thinking on topics ranging from business strategy and visual thinking to organisational design and the challenges of modern leadership. Built on the Blot platform with Obsidian-style formatting, the wiki appears to be part of Philpin's broader ecosystem of writing that includes his main website at philpin.com and his Substack newsletter "Human By Design."

The content reflects Philpin's perspective on how traditional business structures and frameworks are becoming inadequate in today's rapidly changing world. His work explores the evolution from visual thinking concepts (coined in the 1960s) to more sophisticated approaches for communicating complex ideas and solving problems in business contexts. Through the wiki and his other platforms, Philpin shares insights on moving beyond rigid organisational charts and KPIs toward more adaptive, human-centered approaches that recognise the importance of 'soft skills'"' and contextual understanding in modern business environments.
