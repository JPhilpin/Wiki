## What Each Layer Does

### Business Equation (Top)
**Question:** What variables actually move your business?  
**Output:** The "few things that matter" — your strategic focus  
**Used By:** Curation discipline (what signals matter?) + intervention planning (which bottleneck to fix first?)  
**Updated When:** Market changes, competitive landscape shifts, or strategy pivots

### Structured Thought (Clarification Layer)
**Question:** How do we think about these variables systematically?  
**Output:** Frameworks, taxonomies, decision models  
**Used By:** Intelligence (what to extract), Library (how to organize), Conversation (how to discuss)  
**Updated When:** We learn something that changes how we understand the business

### BusinessEngaged (Operating System)
**Question:** How do we turn understanding into impact, and impact back into learning?  
**Output:** The loop. Signal → Wisdom → Impact → Learning → Better Curation  
**Used By:** Everyone. It's the system they work in.  
**Updated When:** The loop itself improves (faster decisions, better outcomes, sharper curation)

### Five Apps (Execution Layer)
**Questions:** 
- Intelligence: What signal matters?
- Library: How do we codify what we know?
- Conversation: What does this mean? What should we do?
- Community: How do we embed wisdom into daily decisions?
- Survey: Did what we predict actually happen?

**Used By:** Teams doing the actual work  
**Updated When:** Processes improve, tools change, capability builds

### DIKW Diagnostic (Measurement Layer)
**Question:** Where are we breaking? What's the bottleneck?  
**Output:** Capability profile + intervention recommendations  
**Used By:** Planning what to fix first  
**Updated When:** Quarterly (or after major interventions)

### Intervention Planning (Action Layer)
**Question:** Given our bottlenecks, what's the highest-impact fix?  
**Output:** Roadmap: Build systems? Train teams? Change culture? Change process?  
**Used By:** Execution teams  
**Updated When:** After each major implementation

### Compound Loop (Continuous)
**Question:** Are we getting better? Is the system tightening?  
**Output:** Improved curation, faster decisions, better outcomes  
**Updated When:** Every cycle (weekly, monthly, quarterly depending on the organization)

## How A Client Uses This

### Week 1: Diagnosis
Client runs DIKW diagnostic. Results show:
- **Right side weak** (can't move from insight to wisdom to action)
- **IMPACT→D broken** (never measure if changes worked)
- **K→I moderate** (can surface insight in conversations, but inconsistently)

**Diagnosis:** "You understand your business well. You struggle to act on understanding, and you don't learn from outcomes."

### Week 2-4: Planning
Using Business Equation + Structured Thought, we define:
- What 3 decisions actually move your needle?
- How do we know when we've made them?
- What insight would trigger each decision?

**Output:** Intervention plan. Build Conversation + Survey capability first. Fix I→W and IMPACT→D.

### Week 5-12: Implementation
We build:
1. **Conversation protocol** — Structured way to move from insight to decision
2. **Survey discipline** — Measurement before change, tracking after
3. **Learning cycles** — Monthly: "Did we predict right? Why/why not?"

**BusinessEngaged in action:** Signals aggregate → Curation surfaces insights → Conversation embeds wisdom → Decision made → Survey measures impact → Learning feeds back into next curation cycle

### Month 4+: Compounding
As the loop tightens:
- Curation gets sharper (you know what matters)
- Decisions happen faster (less debate, clearer process)
- Impact improves (you're making better bets)
- Learning accelerates (feedback is immediate)

The system compounds. Measurement at every node feeds back into better aggregation.

## Where BusinessEngaged Fits

It's **not** the Business Equation. (That's what you decide.)  
It's **not** Structured Thought. (That's how you think about what you decide.)  
It's **not** the five apps. (Those are tools that run on it.)  
It's **not** the diagnostic. (That measures whether it's working.)

**It's the system that operationalizes the equation, embeds the thought discipline, runs the apps, and gives you something to measure.**

## Key Insight

Most organizations have:
- **Equation work** — Strategy, business planning (often good)
- **Thought work** — Frameworks, models (often incomplete)
- **App tools** — Dashboards, documents, channels, communities (often disconnected)
- **No operating system** — No discipline connecting signals to decisions to outcomes to learning

They buy intelligence tools and expect magic. Intelligence alone is useless without curation. Curation is useless without syndication. Syndication is useless without measurement. Measurement is useless without learning.

**BusinessEngaged is the system that forces all of it to happen together.**

## Common Mistake

**Don't start with tools.** Start with the equation. Build Structured Thought. Then layer in BusinessEngaged discipline. Then the tools fit.

If you start with "Let's get a better dashboard," you're missing the point. The dashboard (Intelligence app) is one node. By itself, it changes nothing.

If you start with "What decisions move our needle?" and "How do we systematically surface wisdom to drive those decisions?" then the dashboard becomes part of something that works.

---

## Implementation Sequence (Recommended)

1. **Define Business Equation** — What 3-5 decisions actually matter?
2. **Run DIKW Diagnostic** — Where do you break?
3. **Pick one transition to fix** — Usually IMPACT→D (close the learning loop first)
4. **Build that transition** — One app at a time. Usually: Survey + Measurement first
5. **Tighten the loop** — Use new data to improve curation
6. **Fix the next transition** — Let the system reveal which one breaks next
7. **Repeat** — The system compounds

Don't try to build all five apps at once. That fails. Build one transition, then let it reveal the next.
