This web property is a file structure of markdown files and png images with occasional scripts all stored on iCloud. The vast majority of what you see is written and managed by **Obsidian** - but that same file structure is also shared with Blot that renders the site you are looking at. 

All very 'simple'.

- **Obsidian** - tiny company of what(?) - 5 people - does what it says on the tin.
- **Blot** - tiny company of 1 (David Merfield) - does what it says on the tin - and when it doesn’t - it gets fixed. (Thank you David)
- **iCloud** - part of 🍎 (an enormous company) - kinda of does what it says on the tin - but boy - their syncing is definitely problematic - go figure.

**I guess 'small is beautiful'** ❓

**Styling** is taking shape - though having started a restructure of the CSS a week ago - there are a few known broken elements 

And then over this past weekend I have moved the logic of the material on the wiki to be more '[[Pages/Home Page Links/Zettelkasten|Zettelkasten]] like'. Its a practice that I have long admired as concept - but one that I have also got very little understanding of - but it sure does appeal to my brain. Suffice to say - amateur at the wheel of this vehicle - but if life isn't about learning - then what is it?
