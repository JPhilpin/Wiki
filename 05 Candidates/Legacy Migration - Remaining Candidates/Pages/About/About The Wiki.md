This wiki began as a folder of notes, half-formed thoughts, and ideas gathered from years of conversations, books, and projects.  
Over time, those fragments started to reveal a pattern - a way of seeing how people, systems, and technology intersect.  
What you’re reading here is the early structure of that pattern.
## Structured Thought
At the centre sits **[[Structured Thought]]** - the discipline that underpins everything I work on, from Philpin to Vantaset.  
It’s part mindset, part toolkit: a framework for turning complexity into clarity, and for keeping people at the centre of performance, innovation, and change.

> [Structured Thought is how we turn complexity into clarity - not by simplifying, but by seeing what connects.
## Inside the Wiki

- **Ideas** - early sketches of models and lists (*The Ds*, *The Cs*, *The Ts*) that frame how I think about purpose, capability, and context.  
- **People** - short notes on the thinkers who’ve shaped this work: Geoffrey Moore, Dee Hock, Dave Snowden, Harold Leavitt, Stewart Brand, Mike Maples, Simon Wardley, and others.  
- **Elements** - the building blocks of every organisation: *People, Process, Technology, Value, Offer, Money,* and *Things.*  
- **Books** - distilled insights from *Crossing the Chasm*, *Zone to Win*, *Pattern Breakers*, and *The Tipping Point.*  
- **Podcasts** - transcripts and reflections from recorded conversations about design, leadership, and performance.  
- **Commentary** - essays and fragments exploring what Structured Thinking means and why it matters.  
- **Admin** - lists, scaffolds, and other behind-the-scenes material that keeps the rest organised.

## A Living Record

Nothing here is final. Some pages are complete; others are only sentences. That’s intentional.  
Thought develops in public -  slowly, unevenly, sometimes contradicting itself.  
This wiki exists to catch that process in motion.

It isn’t meant to impress or to sell.  
It’s a map of exploration: ideas meeting practice, old wisdom meeting new systems, and the ongoing search for better ways to work together.

*This page is part of an evolving projectvupdated periodically as the work, and the thinking, continue to grow.*
