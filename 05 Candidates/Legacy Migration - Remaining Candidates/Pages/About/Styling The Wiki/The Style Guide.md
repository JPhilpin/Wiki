### Text Formatting

**BOLD  ur semper. Nullam ut metus quam. Suspendisse potenti**


*ITALICS ur semper. Nullam ut metus quam. Suspendisse potenti*


==HIGHLIGHT ur semper. Nullam ut metus quam. Suspendisse potenti.==


~~STRIKE OUT ur semper. Nullam ut metus quam. Suspendisse potenti.~~


UNDERLINE - Not supported in Obsidian?


`CODE ur semper. Nullam ut metus quam. Suspendisse potenti.`

## We Have Three Different Link Styles On The Wiki

A Link to another wiki page: [[Structured Thinking/📙 Definitions/Chain|Chain]] - with hover

A Link to an external page: [A Philpin Site](https://philpin.com) - with hover

A Link to an external page: [NOT a Philpin Site - e.g. wikipedia](https://wikipedia.org) - with red hover - reminding you that you are bout to leave - not just the Wiki - but the Philpin Domain.
