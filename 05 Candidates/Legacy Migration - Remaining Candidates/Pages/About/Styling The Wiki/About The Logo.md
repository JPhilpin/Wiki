Is the logo just a device - or does it mean something?

It means something - **it's my name!**

Beyond that, this new 'fork in the road' is building on the last 15 years of my work where I have had a focus on [[Structured Thinking/Concepts/People First]] - which remains the **why** of my work. The **how** emerges from something I call [[Structured Thinking/Commentary/Structured Thinking]]. [[Structured Thinking/Concepts/People First]] emerged from the single idea that the world is rushing blindly into '[[Structured Thinking/Concepts/Tech First]]' Thinking ... it was clear 15 years ago. Fast Forward and with the advent of AI there is a great awakening - and society is gradually realising that things might have got a little out of hand.

[[Structured Thinking/1 Atoms/Harold Leavitt]] introduced the world to the '[[Structured Thinking/🕋 Models/Diamond Model]]' in the 60s - which included the phrase [[Structured Thinking/Commentary/People and Process and Technology|People - Process - Technology]] - or as I say ... 

> People. Process. **Then** Technology ...

<p class="attribution">💬 Harold Leavitt (with a small refinement)</p>

[[Structured Thinking/Concepts/People First|People First]] traces its mission and lineage directly the that line and so PHI\PIN was born ... **where technology and people meet** ...

![[Invisibles/_Images/pf-tenets/people-process-technology.png]]

**P**eople, **H**umanity and **I**ndividuals converging around **PHI (φ)** ... **the mathematical constant that creates 'perfect' proportion** and **'PIN'** - In our digital age, nothing matters more than your 'Personal Identification Number' - lose it, and you vanish from the system.

![[Invisibles/_Images/wiki-footers/philpin-logo-black.png]]
