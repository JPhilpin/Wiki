At its core, *Crossing the Chasm* is about how breakthrough technologies move from early enthusiasts to the mainstream market - and how most fail trying. Geoffrey Moore's key insight is that there's a dangerous gap (the "chasm") between visionary early adopters, who buy on potential, and pragmatic early majority buyers, who buy on proof. The essence of the book is learning to focus: narrow your target, dominate a niche, create tangible evidence of success, and only then scale. It's a playbook for turning promise into sustainable momentum.
