Gladwell's core insight: ideas spread like epidemics through three interdependent roles. Each brings something essential. Remove one, and momentum stalls.

## Mavens: The Knowledge Accumulators

Mavens are data banks. They gather, process, and share useful information. People turn to them for advice because they're reliably knowledgeable.

In epidemics of ideas, Mavens provide the substance and credibility of the message.

## Connectors: The Social Glue

Connectors are social hubs. They know large numbers of people across different circles. They bridge groups that wouldn't normally connect, creating the pathways through which ideas travel fast and far.

Connectors ensure the message reaches diverse populations.

## Communicators: The Persuaders

Communicators are charismatic persuaders. They have the skill to convince people even when they're skeptical. They frame ideas in ways that resonate and motivate action.

Communicators energize and drive adoption of new ideas.

## How They Work Together

These three roles are interdependent. None works in isolation:

- Mavens provide substance without reach.
- Connectors spread the word but need credible content.
- Communicators persuade but need authentic information and distribution.

Together, they create the conditions for an idea to "tip" - to reach critical mass and spread as a social epidemic.

**Caveat:** People rarely fit neatly into one category. Most of us carry elements of all three. But individuals tend to gravitate toward one role based on their natural disposition, their position in networks, and the dynamics of those around them.

## Connectors, Communicators and Mavens vs. Hipsters, Hustlers and Nerds

Two related but distinct frameworks. Connectors, Communicators and Mavens describe how ideas spread through networks and populations—social diffusion. Hipsters, Hustlers and Nerds describe who builds and drives product execution inside organisations—execution:

- Hipsters: design, brand, product feel
- Hustlers: sales, growth, market traction
- Nerds: tech, code, engineering

Together, they create an inside-outside balance. One explains social epidemics; the other explains how to build the thing people will adopt.

## Why This Matters

This model is foundational to **People First** thinking in organisational team building. When you understand these three roles, you stop trying to find one superhero and start orchestrating a triad. You recognise that growth acceleration requires all three, that missing one creates a ceiling.
