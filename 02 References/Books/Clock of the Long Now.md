Published in 1999 with the subtitle *Time and Responsibility*. The book builds on two essays: Danny Hillis's "The Millennium Clock" and [[Brian Eno]]'s "The Big Here and Long Now". It sets out the case for thinking and acting on a ten thousand year horizon, which became [[Long Now|The Long Now]].

## Pace Layers in the book

The Pace Layers diagram debuted here, captioned as the order of a healthy civilisation, where the fast layers innovate, the slow layers stabilise and the whole combines learning with continuity. Brand's 2018 essay on the idea is a slightly edited version of a chapter from this book. See [[Pace Layers]].
