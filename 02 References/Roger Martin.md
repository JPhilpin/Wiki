Roger Martin is a Canadian academic, author, and advisor who served as Dean of the University of Toronto's Rotman School of Management for fifteen years. His concept of integrative thinking - the ability to hold two conflicting ideas in mind simultaneously and resolve the tension into a better idea than either alone - challenges the binary either/or logic that dominates most strategic decisions.

His book Playing to Win (co-authored with A.G. Lafley) translated integrative thinking into a practical strategy framework built around two questions: where will you play, and how will you win? It remains one of the clearest articulations of what strategy actually means in practice - a deliberate choice to do some things and not others, rather than a plan to do everything slightly better.
