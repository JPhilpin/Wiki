This page covers only the part of Eno's work that Structured Thought draws on. For the rest of his career, start at the profiles above.

## The Long Now

Eno is a co-founder and board member of The Long Now Foundation, and he named it. His essay "The Big Here and Long Now" is one of the two essays behind *[[Clock of the Long Now|The Clock of the Long Now]]*. His view is that the present is never just a moment: it grows out of the past and seeds the future. He also holds that civilisations with a longer now look after things better.

## Pace Layers

Eno shaped the wording of [[Pace Layers]]. After a conversation with him, [[Stewart Brand]] renamed the top layer from Art to Fashion and the Government layer to Governance. Brand opens his Pace Layers essay with Eno's remark about long nows and then offers the layers as the mechanism behind it. Eno also wrote the music for the BBC series based on Brand's *How Buildings Learn*.
