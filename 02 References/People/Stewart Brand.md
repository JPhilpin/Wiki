This page covers only the part of Brand's work that Structured Thought draws on. For the rest of his life and work, start at his own site, [stewartbrand.org](https://stewartbrand.org/).

## Pace Layers

His best-known idea describes a civilisation as a stack of layers moving at different speeds: fashion moves fast, governance slowly, nature barely at all. It grew from the architect Frank Duffy's layers of a building, which Brand expanded in *How Buildings Learn*, and took its civilisation form in a sketch made after working it through with [[Brian Eno]]. [[Paul Saffo]] uses and teaches it. His essay "Pace Layering: How Complex Systems Learn and Keep Learning" proposes six levels of pace and size. Its central claim is that the contradictions between layers are where a system finds its health, because each layer corrects the others. The full model, with the six layers, is on the [[Pace Layers]] page.

## Work the wiki draws on

- **[[Long Now|The Long Now Foundation]]** - Co-founded with Danny Hillis to think and act on a 10,000 year horizon. Home of the 10,000 Year Clock, being built inside a mountain in West Texas.
- **[[Clock of the Long Now|The Clock of the Long Now]]** - The book that makes the case for the Foundation, and where the Pace Layers diagram first appeared.
- **[[Long Bets]]** - Public, accountable predictions with a charity stake, co-founded with Kevin Kelly.
- **[[Whole Earth Catalog]]** - Oversized reviews of tools, books and ideas for self-directed living. The Last Whole Earth Catalog won the National Book Award in Contemporary Affairs and sold 1.5 million copies.
