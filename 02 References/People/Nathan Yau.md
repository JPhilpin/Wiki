Nathan Yau is a statistician and data visualisation expert best known for founding FlowingData, a platform dedicated to helping people understand data through visual storytelling. With a PhD in Statistics from UCLA, Yau's research examined how non-experts could work with their own personal data more effectively—an inquiry that led directly to his life's work: democratising data literacy through accessible visualisation.

**Yau's contribution to Structured Thought is the principle that visibility is the gateway to understanding.** Through FlowingData and his books Visualize This and Data Points, he demonstrates that people do not need statistical training to see patterns, relationships, and stories within data—they need visual clarity. By making data visible, Yau makes the invisible not just apparent but comprehensible. This is precisely what Structured Thought requires: evidence and insight rendered in a form that ordinary people can grasp, question, and act upon.

His approach combines practical design guidance with statistical rigour, teaching practitioners how to gather, parse, and format data before designing graphics that reveal rather than obscure. His work spans traditional print, interactive web-based visualisations, and experimental data projects, demonstrating across media that the constraint of clarity—in colour, layout, interaction—sharpens understanding.

Through his writing, teaching, and the ongoing work of FlowingData, Yau has made data visualisation a discipline available to journalists, analysts, designers, and anyone committed to making data work intelligibly.
