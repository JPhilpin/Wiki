Stowe Boyd is an information technologist and researcher who has spent decades thinking about how networked tools change the way people work and relate. He is widely credited with coining the terms 'advisory capital' and 'social tools', and in 2007 coined the word 'hashtag' - one of the most consequential pieces of naming in the history of the internet.

His broader work on the social web and the [[Future of Work]] anticipates many of the themes that Structured Thinking takes as foundational: that the shift from systems of record to systems of engagement is not primarily a technology change, but a social one. People do not adopt tools because they are available - they adopt them when the tools fit how they actually want to work.
