Stuart Robbins spent over 20 years as an IT manager in Silicon Valley before distilling his experience into a breakthrough insight: information systems are mirrors of the people who build them. This observation, core to his book *Lessons in Grid Computing: The System Is a Mirror*, suggests that organizational problems embedded in technology are fundamentally human problems in disguise.

His work echoes a central theme in Structured Thinking—that systems don't fail because of the code or architecture, but because the thinking, culture, and assumptions of the people building them are incomplete or misaligned. The system reveals what we didn't know we believed.
