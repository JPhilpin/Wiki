Stewart Brand is an American writer, editor, and cultural provocateur who has been at the centre of technology and counterculture thinking since the 1960s. He founded the Whole Earth Catalog in 1968 and has been connecting ideas across disciplines ever since.

His Pace Layers model - developed with architect Frank Duffy and elaborated with Paul Saffo - describes civilisation as a stack of layers moving at different speeds: fashion moves fast, governance moves slow, nature barely moves at all. The model is essential for understanding why organisations struggle to change: different parts of them operate at incompatible speeds, and most change programmes try to move everything at once.
