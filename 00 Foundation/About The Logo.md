Is the logo just a device, or does it mean something?

It means something. **It is my name.**

Beyond that, this new 'fork in the road' builds on the last 15 years of my work, where the focus has been [[People First]]. That remains the **why** of my work. The **how** emerges from something I call [[Structured Thought]]. People First emerged from a single idea: that the world is rushing blindly into 'Tech First' thinking. It was clear 15 years ago. Fast forward, and with the advent of AI there is a great awakening. Society is gradually realising that things might have got a little out of hand.

[[Harold Leavitt]] introduced the world to the [[Diamond Model]] in the 1960s, which included the phrase [[People, Process THEN Technology|People - Process - Technology]]. Or, as I say:

> People. Process. **Then** Technology.

<p class="attribution">💬 Harold Leavitt (with a small refinement)</p>

[[People First]] traces its mission and lineage directly to that line, and so PHI⑊PIN was born: **where technology and people meet**.

![[people-process-technology.png]]

**P**eople, **H**umanity and **I**ndividuals converge around **PHI (φ)**, **the mathematical constant that creates 'perfect' proportion**. And **PIN**: in our digital age, nothing matters more than your Personal Identification Number. Lose it, and you vanish from the system.

![PHI⑊PIN](https://philpin.com/resources/logos/philpin-logo-lscape-master.jpeg)

## But why the slash?

The backslash has an inherently technological feel. Visually, it suggests a different kind of connection (more integrated than a simple 'or' or 'and') and reveals a potentially new path between people and technology.
