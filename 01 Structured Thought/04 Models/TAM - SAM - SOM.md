# TAM / SAM / SOM

The **TAM / SAM / SOM** model provides a progressively narrower view of market opportunity.

**Total Addressable Market (TAM)** represents the total potential market if every possible customer were served.

**Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)** identifies the portion of that total market that can be served by the relevant offer, capability, geography or operating model.

**Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)** identifies the portion of the serviceable market that can realistically be captured under current or anticipated conditions.

Together, the three levels distinguish theoretical opportunity from addressable opportunity and realistic opportunity. The value of the Model lies in making those differences visible rather than allowing a single large market number to stand in for all three.

## Relationship

**TAM → SAM → SOM**

The terms themselves belong in the Glossary. This page represents the Model created by using them together.
