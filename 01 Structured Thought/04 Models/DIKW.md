## DIKW

The DIKW hierarchy is the foundational model for understanding how raw material becomes useful intelligence.

- **[[Data]]** — unprocessed facts. Numbers, signals, observations. Meaningless without context.
- **[[Information]]** — data processed and contextualised. Patterns emerge. Questions become answerable.
- **[[Knowledge]]** — intelligence understood in depth. You don't just know the answer, you understand why.
- **[[Wisdom]]** — knowledge applied with judgment. The long view. The ethical weight. The decision that holds up over time.

Each level transforms the one below it. None is sufficient alone.
