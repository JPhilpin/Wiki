# Structured Thought

## Design Journal

### July 2026

*A working record of the conversations, architectural discoveries and design decisions that are shaping Structured Thought.*

---

# Purpose

This document captures the evolution of the ideas behind Structured Thought before they are distilled into the Blueprint.

Nothing here is considered Canon.

Its purpose is to preserve the thinking, the experiments, the questions and the architectural discoveries that led to the eventual framework.

---

# Today's Major Discovery

Perhaps the biggest architectural change today was recognising that **People First is not the top of the hierarchy**.

For some time we had unconsciously been allowing People First to become the umbrella for everything.

The discussion revealed that this was asking too much of one philosophy.

Instead we recognised three distinct layers.

```text
PHI⑊PIN

↓

Structured Thought

↓

People First
```

Each serves a different purpose.

---

## PHI⑊PIN

PHI⑊PIN represents the intellectual estate.

It is the publisher, the umbrella and the long-term home for the work.

It is not itself a methodology.

It is not itself a philosophy.

It is the place from which those things are published.

---

## Structured Thought

Structured Thought emerged today as the true operating methodology.

It is the design language.

It provides the models through which ideas become systems, systems become applications and applications create meaningful human outcomes.

Everything technical, organisational and architectural naturally belongs here.

---

## People First

People First remains fundamental.

However, it is now understood as a guiding philosophy within Structured Thought rather than the root of the entire body of work.

It informs every system.

It does not replace the methodology.

---

```text

        │

        ▼

Structured Thought

        ├── Business Models

        ├── Systems

        ├── Domains
```

This hierarchy immediately resolved several previous uncertainties.

# Systems

Today's discussion clarified that Structured Thought contains a family of systems rather than a single platform.

Examples include

- Systems of Record
- Systems of Intelligence
- Systems of Engagement™
- Future Systems yet to be defined

Each system may contain many applications.

---

# Systems of Engagement™

A significant shift occurred during today's discussion.

Originally the Engagement Profile™ appeared to be the centre of the work.

Instead we realised it is simply one application inside a much larger System of Engagement™.

The Engagement Profile™ measures one aspect of engagement.

The broader system is concerned with designing better human engagement across work, organisations and society.

---

# Application Architecture

Rather than creating one increasingly complicated application, we recognised that a chain of small applications is considerably more powerful.

Each application produces structured output.

That output becomes the input to the next application.

For example

```text
Context

↓

Engagement Profile™

↓

Team

↓

Organisation

↓

Portfolio

↓

Enterprise

↓

Network
```

Every application communicates using structured JSON.

Applications remain independent.

No application owns another.

Each performs one job well.

---

# Context Engine

One of today's biggest design discoveries was the Context Engine.

Rather than asking people to complete an assessment in isolation, every assessment begins with the context of the work.

Questions explored included

- What stage is the work in?
- What outcomes are required?
- How much exploration versus governance is needed?
- What leadership style is appropriate?
- What delivery model is appropriate?

The Context Engine produces structured JSON.

The Engagement Profile consumes it.

Future applications will consume the same structure.

---

# Context Before Personality

An important philosophical decision emerged.

The assessment should never attempt to change an individual's natural profile.

Instead it should measure the relationship between

- the work
- the person
- the team

The output therefore becomes

Alignment.

Stretch.

Potential friction.

Potential blind spots.

Potential strengths.

This is a fundamentally different objective from traditional personality assessments.

---

# Team Assessment

Rather than averaging personalities together, the Team application compares individual engagement profiles against the demands of the project.

The output becomes

- team shape
- capability distribution
- strengths
- gaps
- likely tensions
- likely blind spots
- recommendations

This is an assessment of fit rather than judgement.

---

# Project Shape

One particularly exciting idea emerged.

Projects themselves can be plotted.

Individuals can be plotted.

Entire teams can then be layered over the project shape.

The discussion moved away from quadrant thinking towards viewing projects as shapes.

Every project becomes a multidimensional profile.

Every team becomes another profile.

The degree of overlap becomes immediately visible.

---

# Visualisation

Rather than reducing results to a single archetype, visualisations should reveal relationships.

Ideas discussed included

- scatter plots
- project overlays
- heat maps
- centre of gravity
- cluster analysis
- outliers
- concentric confidence rings

The emphasis is on understanding systems rather than classifying people.

---

# The Four Dimensions

The existing Engagement Profile remains the foundation.

Create ↔ Maintain

Quest ↔ Groove

Architect ↔ Gardener

Guide ↔ Listener

These dimensions are viewed as behavioural tendencies rather than fixed personality traits.

They describe how people naturally engage with work.

---

# Dynamic Context

Initially we considered free-text project descriptions.

After discussion we recognised that guided inputs produce more reliable prototypes.

Possible approaches include

- weighted sliders
- percentage allocations
- guided scenarios
- structured prompts

Free-form AI interpretation remains a future enhancement.

---

# JSON Philosophy

Applications communicate only through structured data.

The Context Engine exports JSON.

The Team application imports JSON.

Future applications consume the same structure.

This creates a modular ecosystem rather than a monolithic application.

---

# Domains

The original domains remain valid.

- Data
- Identity
- Technology
- Work
- Commerce
- Learning
- Values
- Language

However they are now understood as Domains within Structured Thought rather than People First pillars.

---

# Studio and Canon

Today's conversation produced what may become one of the defining concepts.

Studio is where thinking evolves.

Canon is where thinking stabilises.

Knowledge should always mature through Studio before promotion.

The same lifecycle applies to documents, models, systems and applications.

---

# Obsidian

The decision was made to begin inside Studio.

The existing Wiki remains intact.

Studio becomes the active workspace.

Canon will emerge gradually.

This mirrors the intellectual lifecycle itself.

---

# Editorial Principle

An important principle emerged.

Nothing important should ever be lost.

Ideas may

- evolve
- merge
- simplify
- move

but they should never silently disappear.

This principle will guide the development of both Studio and Canon.

---

# Looking Ahead

Today's work fundamentally changed the direction of the project.

We no longer see ourselves as building an engagement assessment.

We are building a coherent intellectual framework.

That framework contains

- philosophies
- methodologies
- domains
- systems
- applications
- research
- publications

Each supporting the others.
