# The Engagement Profile™
## Positioning Paper
### From Individual Assessment to Context-Driven Team Design

## The Opportunity

Most workplace assessments begin with the individual.

They ask:

- Who are you?
- What is your personality?
- How do you prefer to work?

They then attempt to infer how a collection of individuals might function as a team.

The Engagement Profile™ takes a different approach.

It recognises that people rarely fail because they are the "wrong type". More often, they struggle because the demands of the work have changed while the composition of the team has remained static.

The fundamental question therefore becomes:

> **What does this work require, and how well does this team fit those requirements?**

This changes the assessment from describing people to designing teams.

---

# The Philosophy

The Engagement Profile™ is not a personality assessment.

It is a collaboration framework.

It measures how people naturally engage with work across four continuous dimensions:

- Create ↔ Maintain
- Quest ↔ Groove
- Architect ↔ Gardener
- Guide ↔ Listener

These are not fixed labels.

They represent preferred ways of contributing to work, recognising that individuals may adapt over time and across different contexts.

The individual profile remains constant.

The context changes.

The relationship between the two creates insight.

---

# The Next Evolution

The current Engagement Profile™ produces an individual profile.

The next stage extends this into a context-driven team design platform.

Rather than asking only:

> "Who is on the team?"

the platform first asks:

> "What does this piece of work require?"

The context becomes the reference point against which every individual profile is compared.

---

# A Modular Platform

The platform is intentionally modular.

Each application performs a specific role and exports structured data to the next application.

## Application 1 — Individual Engagement Profile

### Purpose

Measure an individual's natural engagement preferences.

### Output

A structured JSON profile containing:

- Individual scores
- Archetype
- Dimension positions
- Supporting metadata

No judgement is made about suitability for any particular project.

---

## Application 2 — Team Design

### Purpose

Design the best team for a specific piece of work.

### Inputs

- Individual Engagement Profile JSON files
- Project Context

### Outputs

- Team shape
- Team distribution
- Context fit
- Gap analysis
- Stretch analysis
- Recommendations
- Team JSON export

The individual profiles remain unchanged.

Only the interpretation changes according to context.

---

# Context Before Assessment

The defining principle of Application 2 is simple.

Every assessment begins by understanding the work itself.

Only then are people compared against it.

This reverses the traditional sequence used by most behavioural assessments.

---

# The Five Ds

The Five Ds describe the lifecycle of the work.

## Discover

Understanding.

Exploring.

Learning.

## Design

Creating possibilities.

Developing concepts.

Testing options.

## Decide

Choosing direction.

Building commitment.

Setting priorities.

## Develop

Building.

Refining.

Delivering.

## Deploy

Implementing.

Embedding.

Scaling.

The stage of the work influences the ideal balance across the four engagement dimensions.

---

# The Three Ts

The Three Ts describe the level of change being created.

They are not project stages.

They are outcomes.

## Transform

**Transform People**

Develop capability.

Strengthen confidence.

Build new behaviours.

Enable individuals to grow.

## Transition

**Transition Organisations**

Change structures.

Improve operating models.

Evolve culture.

Enable organisations to move successfully from one state to another.

## Transcend

**Transcend Markets**

Create new categories.

Redefine expectations.

Move beyond accepted market assumptions.

Shift industries rather than simply compete within them.

Together they express the ambition of the platform:

> **We transform people to transition organisations to transcend markets.**

---

# Mission Fit

Application 2 combines three independent perspectives.

## 1. The Work

Where are we?

- Discover
- Design
- Decide
- Develop
- Deploy

## 2. The Intended Impact

Why are we doing this?

- Transform
- Transition
- Transcend

## 3. The Team

Who do we have available?

- Individual Engagement Profiles
- Roles
- Responsibilities
- Capacity
- Experience

These combine to define the ideal team profile.

The platform then compares the ideal profile with the actual team.

---

# What the Platform Produces

Rather than another personality report, the platform answers practical questions.

- Does this team match the work?
- Which strengths are overrepresented?
- Which capabilities are missing?
- Where will collaboration naturally occur?
- Where is friction likely?
- Which individuals are operating in stretch zones?
- Which additional capabilities would strengthen the team?

The emphasis moves from description to intervention.

---

# Visual Model

The visual model represents both the work and the team.

The project defines the target profile.

Each team member is positioned relative to that target.

The visual immediately reveals:

- Alignment
- Clusters
- Gaps
- Outliers
- Stretch
- Balance

The purpose is not to classify people.

The purpose is to design better teams.

---

# Guiding Principles

The platform will always:

- Preserve the integrity of the individual profile.
- Separate assessment from interpretation.
- Put context before judgement.
- Recognise that work changes faster than people.
- Encourage informed conversation rather than deterministic conclusions.
- Value diversity of contribution over uniformity.
- Design teams around the work rather than forcing the work to fit the team.

---

# Positioning Statement

The Engagement Profile™ is a context-driven collaboration platform that helps organisations understand not only who their people are, but how they can best come together to achieve a specific mission.

It begins with individuals.

It understands the work.

It designs the team.

It enables organisations to transform people, transition organisations, and transcend markets.
