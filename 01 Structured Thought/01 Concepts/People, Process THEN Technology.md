# People, Process THEN Technology

**People, Process THEN Technology** is a deliberate modification of the familiar **People, Process and Technology** formulation.

The distinction is the word **THEN**. Technology should follow an understanding of the people involved and the processes through which work is actually done. Starting with technology tends to force people and process to accommodate the tool rather than asking the tool to support the work.

The original People, Process and Technology formulation is commonly associated with Harold Leavitt's work on organisational change. Structured Thought retains the three elements but makes their order explicit.

## Composition

**People → Process → THEN Technology**
