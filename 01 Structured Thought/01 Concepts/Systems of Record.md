# Systems of Record

## Working definition

A concept describing systems whose primary role is to maintain authoritative records of transactions, entities, events or organisational state.

Within the Business Equation, Systems of Record represent the record, compliance and operational side of the organisation. They remain essential, but their historic control over information and supply does not by itself create engagement or responsiveness.
