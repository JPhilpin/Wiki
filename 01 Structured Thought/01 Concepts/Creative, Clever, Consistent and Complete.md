# Creative, Clever, Consistent and Complete

**Creative, Clever, Consistent and Complete** brings four distinct Primitives together as a practical test of the quality of an idea, solution or piece of work.

**Creative** asks whether something opens useful possibilities rather than merely repeating what already exists.

**Clever** asks whether the idea demonstrates insight and makes intelligent use of the conditions available to it.

**Consistent** asks whether the idea holds together across its different expressions and continues to make sense when applied repeatedly.

**Complete** asks whether the work has been carried far enough to function as intended rather than remaining an attractive fragment.

None of the four is sufficient alone. Creativity without consistency can become novelty. Cleverness without completion remains unrealised. Consistency without creativity can preserve the wrong answer. Completion without the other qualities can simply make a weak idea permanent.

## Composition

**Creative + Clever + Consistent + Complete**
