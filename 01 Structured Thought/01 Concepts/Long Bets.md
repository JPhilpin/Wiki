**Website:** [longbets.org](https://longbets.org/)

**Run by:** [[Long Now|The Long Now Foundation]]

**Co-founders:** [[Stewart Brand]] and Kevin Kelly

Long Bets exists to improve long-term thinking. Every bet on the site names a predictor, a challenger, a stake and a duration, all on the public record.

## How it works

- Anyone can make a prediction.
- Anyone can challenge a prediction with a bet.
- The winner names the charity that receives the money.
- The Long Now Foundation provides the continuity to carry even the longest bets through to public resolution.
- The site is also a forum for discussing what the bets and their outcomes teach.

## An example

Martin Rees predicted that a bioterror or bioerror event would cause one million casualties within a six-month period, starting no later than the end of 2020. Steven Pinker took the other side.
