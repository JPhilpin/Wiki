# The Business Equation

**The Business Equation** provides a high-level context for understanding how a business creates value and how that context is changing.

At its core, any business combines **People, Money and Things**, applies capability, process and judgement, and creates value that other people or organisations are willing to exchange for.

The Equation also frames a larger transition in where business power resides. Producer-led organisations historically optimised around supply, control, efficiency and systems of record. Increasingly, customers, networks, engagement and live demand signals shape how value is created and delivered.

This transition is expressed through two related Concepts:

**Producer Efficient Supply Chain (PESC) → Customer Effective Demand Network (CEDN)**

The Business Equation is therefore not a formula in the mathematical sense. It is a contextual map for examining where value comes from, how it moves and where attention should be placed as business conditions change.

## The Shift in Business Power

The Business Equation was developed to make visible a broader change in how businesses operate. Historically, power tended to reside in the **Systems of Record** hemisphere because control of records, compliance and supply supported control of the supply chain.

The transition from **Producer Efficient Supply Chains** to **Customer Effective Demand Networks** changes that balance. In a world of greater abundance and connectivity, supply alone is less defensible. Engagement, attention and responsiveness become increasingly important sources of advantage.

This does not make operational efficiency irrelevant. It changes the question. Discovery must distinguish symptoms from causes and identify where value, attention and decision-making power are actually moving.
