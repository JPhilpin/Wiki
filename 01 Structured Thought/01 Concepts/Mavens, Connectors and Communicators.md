# Mavens, Connectors and Communicators

**Mavens, Connectors and Communicators** describes three complementary roles that help knowledge and ideas move through organisations and networks.

The Concept draws on Malcolm Gladwell's distinction between Mavens, Connectors and Salesmen, but deliberately uses **Communicators** as the third role within Structured Thought.

**Mavens** gather, understand and share knowledge. They provide substance, expertise and informed judgement.

**Connectors** build and maintain relationships across groups, disciplines and communities. They create the paths through which people and ideas can meet.

**Communicators** shape and convey ideas so that they can be understood, remembered and acted upon. They translate complexity into language appropriate to the audience and context.

The roles are complementary rather than hierarchical. Mavens provide substance, Connectors create reach, and Communicators make meaning transferable. An individual may perform more than one role, but distinguishing them helps reveal where a team or network has strength and where it has gaps.

## Composition

**Mavens + Connectors + Communicators**
