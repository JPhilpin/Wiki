# People, Money and Things

**People, Money and Things** is a deliberately simple way of describing the fundamental resources available to an organisation.

**People** contribute capability, judgement, relationships, knowledge and action.

**Money** provides a transferable representation of economic value and the means to acquire, allocate and exchange resources.

**Things** covers the physical and non-physical resources that are neither people nor money: products, materials, information, intellectual property, infrastructure, technology and other assets.

The usefulness of the Concept lies in its simplicity. Whatever the organisation does, it must organise some combination of People, Money and Things and transform that combination into something of greater value.

This makes the Concept a foundational component of **The Business Equation**.

## Composition

**People + Money + Things**
