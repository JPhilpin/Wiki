**Glossary definition:** [Intelligence](/glossary/intelligence/)
**Status:** 🔴 written - not read

> [!quote] You are not suffering from a lack of information. You are drowning in it.

> [!question] You might think it's...
> An aggregator. A newsletter roundup. A really good RSS feed.
>
> It isn't. An aggregator collects. Intelligence curates. Curation means knowing what to include and what to leave out, scoring content for credibility, freshness, and relevance, and filtering out the noise that looks like signal but isn't.

## What It Means

News feeds, newsletters, reports, updates, announcements. It arrives constantly, in formats you did not ask for, on topics you may or may not care about. You skim, you bookmark, you mean to go back. You rarely do. The result is not ignorance, it is noise, and noise is its own kind of blindness. Intelligence takes a domain (a trade corridor, a market sector, a niche you actually care about) and builds a structured, continuously updated picture of what is happening inside it. Not everything. The things that matter.

## The Full Picture

What remains after curation is a focused, trusted stream. From that stream, something starts to happen that cannot happen from raw information alone: you begin to see patterns, connections, movement. Things that were not visible when you were reading everything and understanding nothing. That progression, from raw data to the insight that changes how you act, is not applied after the fact. It is the structural logic behind how Intelligence is built: what gets monitored, how it is scored, how what matters gets surfaced, and how it connects over time so understanding compounds rather than resetting every morning.

This has always been available to large organisations: teams of analysts, research subscriptions, proprietary platforms, at a cost and complexity that put it out of reach for everyone else. Intelligence changes that, not by building new technology, but by thinking differently about what existing technology is for. A small business, a trade association, a niche community can each define a domain, curate a source universe, and build the same kind of structured, compounding, authoritative picture that used to be the exclusive territory of organisations with the budget to afford it.

## How It Fits

Intelligence is the domain that turns noise into something worth acting on. It is foundational in a literal sense: you cannot build knowledge on noise. Reveal has a real question to work from once Intelligence has cut through what does not matter.
