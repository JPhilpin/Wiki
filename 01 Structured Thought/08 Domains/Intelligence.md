# Intelligence

🔴 written - not read

> [!quote] You are not suffering from a lack of information. You are drowning in it.

> [!question] You might think it's...
> An aggregator. A newsletter roundup. A really good RSS feed.
>
> It isn't. An aggregator collects. Intelligence curates. Curation means knowing what to include and what to leave out, scoring content for credibility, freshness, and relevance, and filtering out the noise that looks like signal but isn't.

## What It Means

News feeds, newsletters, reports, updates, announcements. It arrives constantly, in formats you didn't ask for, on topics you may or may not care about. You skim, you bookmark, you mean to go back. You rarely do. The result isn't ignorance, it's noise, and noise is its own kind of blindness. Intelligence takes a domain (a trade corridor, a market sector, a niche you actually care about) and builds a structured, continuously updated picture of what's happening inside it. Not everything. The things that matter.

## The Full Picture

What remains after curation is a focused, trusted stream. From that stream, something starts to happen that can't happen from raw information alone: you begin to see patterns, connections, movement. Things that weren't visible when you were reading everything and understanding nothing. That progression, from raw data to the insight that changes how you act, isn't applied after the fact. It's the structural logic behind how Intelligence is built: what gets monitored, how it's scored, how what matters gets surfaced, and how it connects over time so understanding compounds rather than resetting every morning.

This has always been available to large organisations: teams of analysts, research subscriptions, proprietary platforms, at a cost and complexity that put it out of reach for everyone else. Intelligence changes that, not by building new technology, but by thinking differently about what existing technology is for. A small business, a trade association, a niche community can each define a domain, curate a source universe, and build the same kind of structured, compounding, authoritative picture that used to be the exclusive territory of organisations with the budget to afford it.

## How It Fits

Intelligence is the domain that turns noise into something worth acting on. It's foundational in a literal sense: you can't build knowledge on noise. Reveal has a real question to work from once Intelligence has cut through what doesn't matter. # Definitions

## One Sentence

Intelligence is a curated, continuously updated picture of a domain that turns raw information into the insight that changes how you act.

## One Paragraph

Intelligence takes a domain, a market, a sector, a niche you actually care about, and builds a structured, curated stream from what's happening inside it. Not everything, the things that matter, scored for credibility and relevance and filtered for noise that only looks like signal. What used to require a team of analysts and a research budget is now something a small business or a niche community can build for itself.

## The Long Paragraph

An aggregator collects. Intelligence curates, and the distinction matters more than it sounds. Curation means scoring content across multiple dimensions (how credible the source is, how fresh the signal, how relevant to the domain) and filtering out noise that only resembles signal. What remains is a focused, trusted stream, and from that stream something starts to happen that raw information alone can't produce: patterns, connections, movement become visible. That compounding, from information to the insight that changes how you act, is built into how Intelligence is structured from the start, not bolted on afterwards.
