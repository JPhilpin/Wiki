The eight Domains, where the thinking meets an area of practice.

8 pages.

- **[[Assembly]]** - Domain page for Assembly, a structured space where the people in your domain find each other, not just you.
- **[[Balance]]** - Domain page for Balance, reading whether an exchange of value still holds, whatever currency it is actually running on.
- **[[Engage]]** - Domain page for Engage, the archetype layer that tells every other domain who it is actually talking to.
- **[[Intelligence]]** - Domain page for Intelligence, a curated, continuously updated picture of a domain that turns information into the insight that changes how you act.
- **[[Library]]** - Domain page for Library, curated resources and accumulated thinking organised so they compound rather than disappear.
- **[[Personal]]** - Domain page for Personal, applying the domains framework to your own life with the same rigour you'd bring to a client engagement.
- **[[Pulse]]** - Domain page for Pulse, a structured recurring engagement that tracks what people think over time, not just once.
- **[[Reveal]]** - Domain page for Reveal, a fixed set of questions that turns what people have avoided seeing into a clear reading of where they actually stand.
