# Personal

🔴 written - not read

> [!quote] Every framework applies to organisations. This one applies to you.

> [!question] You might think it's...
> A productivity system. A journalling app. Another way to organise your to-do list.
>
> It isn't. It's the same questions an organisation asks about itself, applied to one person: what do you actually need to follow, who are the people that matter, what do you know versus what are you just storing, and where are you, really.

## What It Means

Most people never ask those questions systematically. They operate on habit, on instinct, on whatever happened to land in front of them today, which is fine until the noise drowns the signal, the connections go unmaintained, the knowledge disappears, and the picture of where you actually are gets blurry. Personal applies the domains framework to your own life, not as a system to manage tasks, but as a structured way of thinking about what you are doing and why.

## The Full Picture

You are not an organisation. But you have a domain, and it deserves the same clarity. Intelligence at the personal level means knowing what you need to follow and filtering everything else. Pulse means tracking the things that matter to you over time, not once, but as a trend. Assembly means maintaining the relationships that compound rather than the ones that merely accumulate. Library means holding what you've learned in a form you can actually use. Reveal means being honest about where you are, not where you wish you were.

None of this requires software. It requires structure, and the willingness to look at your own situation with the same rigour you'd bring to a client engagement. That is harder than it sounds. It is also more useful than almost anything else you could do.

## How It Fits

Personal is the domain that does not appear on any org chart, and it is also the one that determines how well everything else works. Get this right and the other seven domains compound. Leave it unexamined and they fragment, because every other domain is being run, ultimately, by a person who has not applied the same discipline to themselves. *(Provisional.
