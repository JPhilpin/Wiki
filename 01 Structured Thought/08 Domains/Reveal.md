# Reveal

🟠 read - not reviewed

> [!quote] You already know something is wrong. The question is whether you know what.

> [!question] You might think it's...
> A diagnostic. An audit. A scorecard. A survey with better questions.
>
> It isn't. Those tools measure you against criteria someone chose in advance. Which means they confirm what you already thought mattered, and miss what you didn't know to ask. Reveal is built the other way round. The questions come first, fixed and calibrated, designed to surface what you've been unable to see rather than confirm what you already believed.

## What It Means

Most organisations carry a version of the truth they've collectively agreed not to examine too closely. Reveal takes a domain (a business, a team, a go-to-market function, a leadership group) and applies a rigorous set of questions designed to surface what you've been unable to see. The output isn't a report. It's a reading.

## The Full Picture

Reveal maps five dimensions of readiness: clarity of purpose, quality of execution, strength of relationships, capacity for change, and coherence between what you say and what you do. Each is probed through structured questions, each response scored. The aggregate produces a reading: broken, patchy, or strong.

Because the questions are fixed, a reading taken today can be compared to one taken in six months. Progress and regression both become visible. That's when it stops behaving like a one-off audit and starts behaving like a navigation system.

The same engine runs in two directions. Turned inward, it shows an organisation where it's weak, on its own terms. Turned outward, it's how you meet a visitor on theirs. They arrive believing they have problem A, B, or C. Reveal doesn't argue with that. It applies the same structured questioning to show that the real driver is Z, and that Z maps to something they hadn't connected it to. Not "we sell you an XYZ." Solving the problem they actually have, in their language, using their evidence.

## How It Fits

Reveal is the domain that removes agreed fiction (an organisation's fiction about itself, a visitor's fiction about their own problem). Every other domain works better once Reveal has done that. Intelligence has less noise to filter when the real question is already named. Pulse is tracking something true instead of something comfortable. Balance can't tell you if an exchange still holds until Reveal has established what's actually being exchanged. *(Provisional.
