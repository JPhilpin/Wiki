A **Framework** is an organising structure that helps us use understanding to analyse, decide or act.

Frameworks bring relevant Concepts, Principles, Models or other elements into a usable structure. They do more than describe a situation: they help people work with what is understood in a consistent and deliberate way.

Within Structured Thought, a Framework primarily supports application.

**A Model helps you see or understand something. A Framework helps you work with it.**

Actual Frameworks are held in the Frameworks section of Structured Thought.
