## In Structured Thought

In a [[Customer Effective Demand Network]], effectiveness is the organising goal: producing the outcome that matters to the customer. It is measured from the outside, by whether the result is relevant and valued.

Effective is one of the four terms that make up [[CEDN]].

## The mirror

Its opposite number in PESC is **[[Efficient]]**. Efficiency asks whether a thing was made well. Effectiveness asks whether it was the right thing to make.
