**Domain page:** [Personal](/domains/personal/)
**In general:** belonging to, or applied to, an individual's own life, choices and practice rather than an organisation's.

## In Structured Thought

Personal is part of the controlled vocabulary of Structured Thought. Its meaning should be read in relation to the wider system rather than as an isolated dictionary definition.

## Definitions

### One Paragraph

Personal is not a productivity system. It is the same structured questions an organisation asks about itself, asked of one person instead: what actually needs following, who the relationships that matter are, what is genuinely known versus what is just been stored, and whether where you are matches where you think you are. None of it needs software. It needs the willingness to look at your own situation as rigorously as you'd look at a client's.

### The Long Paragraph

Most people never ask these questions systematically. They run on habit and whatever happened to land in front of them that day, which works fine until the noise drowns the signal, the relationships go unmaintained, the knowledge disappears, and the picture of where things actually stand gets blurry. Personal takes the same framework built for organisations and applies it at the individual level: what to follow and what to filter, what to track over time rather than once, which relationships compound rather than merely accumulate, what is actually been learned versus what is just been collected, and an honest read on where things stand rather than where you'd like them to be. It is the domain that does not show up on any org chart, and the one that quietly determines how well every other domain performs.
