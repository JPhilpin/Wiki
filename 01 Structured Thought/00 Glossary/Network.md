## In Structured Thought

In a [[Customer Effective Demand Network]], value moves across a network of customers, communities, partners and platforms. It can flow in many directions, and the network adapts as signals and data move through it.

Network is one of the four terms that make up [[CEDN]].

## The mirror

Its opposite number in PESC is the **[[Chain]]**. A chain is a line in which each link waits on the last; a network is a web that can adapt in several places at once.
