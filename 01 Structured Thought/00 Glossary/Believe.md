# Believe

To hold something as true, possible or worthy of commitment strongly enough for it to shape attention and action.

## In Structured Thought

Believe is part of the controlled vocabulary of Structured Thought. Its meaning should be read in relation to the wider system rather than as an isolated dictionary definition.
