## In Structured Thought

In a [[Producer Efficient Supply Chain]], value moves along a chain: producer, supplier, manufacturer, distributor, customer. It flows mostly in one direction, and each link depends on the one before it.

Chain is one of the four terms that make up [[PESC]].

## The mirror

Its opposite number in CEDN is the **[[Network]]**. A chain is a line; a network is a web, in which value can move in several directions at once.
