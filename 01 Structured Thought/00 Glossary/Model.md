# Model

A **Model** is a representation of something that helps us understand how it is, behaves, relates or might change.

Models simplify aspects of reality so that relationships, assumptions, behaviours and possible consequences can be examined and communicated. They are useful because they make something easier to see without claiming to reproduce the whole of reality.

Within Structured Thought, a Model primarily supports understanding.

**A Model helps you see or understand something. A Framework helps you work with it.**

Actual Models are held in the Models section of Structured Thought.
