## In Structured Thought

Infrastructure is the third of the six [[Pace Layer|pace layers]]. Roads, sewers and communication systems cannot be justified in strictly commercial terms, so [[Governance]] and [[Culture]] have to carry the cost and the long disruption of building them. Education and science count as intellectual infrastructure: the yield is very high and the payback is delayed.
