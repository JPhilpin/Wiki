# Integrity

Alignment between stated values, actual behaviour and the wholeness of the system in which they operate.

## In Structured Thought

Integrity is part of the controlled vocabulary of Structured Thought. Its meaning should be read in relation to the wider system rather than as an isolated dictionary definition.
