Engagement is the wider concept. Engage is the Domain that puts it to work, by knowing who each person actually is.

## In Structured Thought

Engagement is one of the central ideas in the Studio. It sits at the heart of the shift from [[Systems of Record]], which capture what has already happened, to [[Systems of Engagement]], which shape how people take part while it is happening.

A system of record asks whether the data is complete. A system of engagement asks whether people are contributing attention, energy, judgement and action, and whether that participation is improving over time.

Engagement is therefore not a metric to be reported on but a quality to be designed for: in how work is organised, how people are known, and how their contribution is recognised.
