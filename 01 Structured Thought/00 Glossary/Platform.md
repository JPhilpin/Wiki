A platform is a shared foundation on which others can create, connect or exchange value.

## In Structured Thought

In a [[Customer Effective Demand Network]], a platform is a node that connects customers, communities and partners, and carries the data and feedback that allow the network to adapt as conditions change.

Where a supply chain moves products along a line, a platform lets value move in several directions at once.
