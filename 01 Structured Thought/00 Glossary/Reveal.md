## In Structured Thought

Reveal is part of the controlled vocabulary of Structured Thought. Its meaning should be read in relation to the wider system rather than as an isolated dictionary definition.

## Definitions

### One Sentence

Reveal is a set of questions designed to turn what people typically avoid having to see into a clear reading of where they actually stand.

### One Paragraph

Reveal takes a domain (a business, a team, a leadership group, or a visitor's own stated problem) and asks questions. The output is not a report. It is a reading: broken, patchy, or strong. Consistency with the quesitions in each Reveal implementation, a reading taken today can be measured against one taken months later, turning a single snapshot into real progress.

### The Long Paragraph

Reveal maps five dimensions of readiness: clarity of purpose, quality of execution, strength of relationships, capacity for change, and coherence between what you say and what you do. Each is probed through structured questions, each response scored, and together they produce the reading. The same engine runs in two directions. Turned inward, it shows an organisation where it is weak, on its own terms. Turned outward, it is how you meet a visitor on theirs. They arrive believing they have problem A, B, or C. Reveal shows them the real driver is Z, and that Z connects to something they hadn't seen before. Not "we sell you an XYZ." Solving the problem they actually have, in their language, using their evidence.
