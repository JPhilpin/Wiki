## In Structured Thought

Governance is the fourth of the six [[Pace Layer|pace layers]]. Brand separates the public sector (government), the private sector (business) and the social sector (nongovernmental, nonprofit organisations). The social sector acts on culture-level concerns from inside governance. His example is historic preservation, where culture declared that clothes may change at fashion pace but buildings may not. Changing governance suddenly instead of gradually is how revolutions happen.
