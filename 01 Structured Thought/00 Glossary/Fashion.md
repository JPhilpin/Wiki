## In Structured Thought

Fashion is the first and fastest of the six [[Pace Layer|pace layers]], and art sits in it. Its job is froth: it experiments quickly and without responsibility. That variety gives [[Commerce]] its energy, and now and then a good idea sifts down into slower layers such as [[Governance]] and [[Culture]].
