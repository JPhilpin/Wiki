## In Structured Thought

Nature is the sixth and slowest of the six [[Pace Layer|pace layers]]. Its power keeps surprising people: a drought lasting three hundred years ended the world's first empire, and earthquakes and a volcanic eruption ended Europe's first. When people disturb nature at its own scale, as with extinction and greenhouse gases, they risk setting off apocalyptic forces. Brand's conclusion is that people have to comprehend and engage the longest now of nature, which ties this layer to the [[Long Now]].
