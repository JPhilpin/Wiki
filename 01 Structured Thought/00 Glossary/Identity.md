# Identity

The set of characteristics, commitments and relationships through which a person, group or thing is recognised as itself.

## In Structured Thought

Identity is part of the controlled vocabulary of Structured Thought. Its meaning should be read in relation to the wider system rather than as an isolated dictionary definition.
