Visual guides to the territory.

A Map page holds one diagram and lists every page that uses it. The diagram still belongs to its subject - the Business Equation is a Concept and stays one - but the Map page is the canonical home of the image, and the one place that answers where it appears.

19 pages.

- **[[Business Fractalisation]]** - The same shape repeating at every scale of a business.
- **[[Chaord]]** - Chaos and order held together, drawn as a single form.
- **[[Compensation Spectrum]]** - Compensation drawn as a spectrum rather than a set of fixed positions.
- **[[Core and Context]]** - What a business must own against what it can buy in.
- **[[Creative, Clever, Consistent and Complete]]** - The four qualities set against each other.
- **[[Cynefin]]** - Clear, complicated, complex and chaotic - four domains and the disorder between them.

- **[[Eight Domains Map]]** - The eight Domains and their accent colours, shown as one field.
- **[[Five Layers of AI Augmentation]]** - Five layers at which AI augments work.
- **[[Mavens, Connectors and Communicators]]** - The three roles that carry an idea through a network, in seven views.
- **[[PESC and CEDN Mirror]]** - Producer-Efficient-Supply-Chain set against Customer-Effective-Demand-Network, each term facing its opposite.
- **[[People, Process, Technology Map]]** - The three-way relationship between people, process and technology.

- **[[Zone to Win Map]]** - Geoffrey Moore's four zones - Performance, Productivity, Incubation, Transformation.
