## What this is

The Studio is the working home of Structured Thought: the frameworks, models and ideas behind how I think about business and technology, and about the messy middle where people bump into technology.

It is not a finished body of work. It is the place where that work is tested, argued with, revised and occasionally discarded. What you are reading is where the thinking currently stands, not a conclusion.

## Why it is public

Structured Thought makes a claim about how good thinking gets built. Proving that claim inside a sealed notebook would be a contradiction. So the Studio is built in public, including the parts that are not tidy: thin pages, provisional Candidates, and the rules that govern it all.

Some pages are complete. Others are a sentence. Both are deliberate.

## Who builds it

Two entities: John Philpin and Claude, working together.

The thinking, the arguments and the judgement calls are mine. The drafting, structuring and editorial mechanics run through Claude, against rules I have set (see [[Studio Constitution]]). Claude often takes the first pass; I improve on it. Neither replaces the other.

That is a deliberate contrast with [john.philpin.com](https://john.philpin.com), which is written entirely by hand. Here, the collaboration is visible, not hidden.

## Get in touch

Questions, corrections, ideas, or a page you would like to see filled in: email [wikiupdates@philpin.com](mailto:wikiupdates@philpin.com) with the page name, or just say hello. It takes a village.

The wider network lives at [philpin.com](https://philpin.com).
